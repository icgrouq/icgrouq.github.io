HideHist Privacy Policy

Chrome extension "HideHist — Hide Your History" · Last updated: October 29, 2026

HideHist keeps the sites you choose out of your browser history. This policy explains what data the extension uses and why.

In short: no data is sent to the developer or to any server. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.

What the extension accesses

PermissionWhy it is needed
historyTo delete visits to the sites on your list, or all new visits while "Don't save any history" is on.
tabsTo read the address of the current tab, so that "This domain" / "This page" can add it to the list, and to know when you leave a listed site.
storageTo save your list and settings in your browser.
contextMenus, alarmsFor the right-click menu and the "don't save history" timer.
faviconTo show site icons in your list from Chrome's local icon cache. No request is sent to those sites.
bookmarks (optional)Only if you turn on bookmark cleanup: deletes bookmarks of listed sites.
browsingData, downloads (optional)Only if you turn on cookie and download cleanup: clears cookies and site data of listed sites, and removes their entries from the downloads list.

What is stored, and where

  • Your list of sites and your settings are stored locally in your browser.
  • If you turn on Sync in the settings, the list (not the password) is also stored with your Chrome profile's sync data in your Google account, under Google's own privacy policy. Sync is off by default.
  • The optional password is never stored. Only a salted PBKDF2 hash is kept, locally. The password hides the list from people looking at your screen. It does not encrypt the list.

What is not done

  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third-party server.
  • No analytics, tracking, ads or remote code.
  • Your browsing data is never sold or shared.

Actions you start yourself

  • Send to Telegram opens Telegram's public share page (t.me/share/url) in a new tab with the address of the page you chose to share. Nothing is sent until you pick a chat in Telegram.
  • Report a bug opens your own email app with a draft that contains the extension version, browser, operating system and interface language. Your list of sites is never included, and nothing is sent until you press Send.
