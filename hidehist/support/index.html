Questions, ideas and problems

Write to ic.grouq@gmail.com. You can also press Idea or issue in HideHist (More → Help): it opens an email with the extension version, browser and system already filled in. Your list of sites is never included.

Common questions

A site still shows up in history for a moment. Chrome doesn't let extensions stop a visit from being written, so HideHist deletes it right after. It is gone a moment later.

I forgot the password. The password only hides the list. Removing and reinstalling HideHist clears it, together with the list. Export the list first if you can.

I lost my Pro key. Get it again on the restore page with your email and the order number from Paddle's receipt.

Pro on another computer. With Chrome sync on, Pro turns on by itself on your other computers. Otherwise paste the same key there: one purchase works on all your computers.

Refunds. Within 14 days, no questions asked: see the refund policy. Paddle.com, our reseller, handles payments and refunds.
