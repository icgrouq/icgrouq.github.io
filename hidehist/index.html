HideHist — Hide Your History

Chrome extension

Keep the sites you choose out of your browser history, pause history saving completely, wipe the last minutes in a tap, and share any page in one click. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

  • Exception list with domains, pages, patterns and searches
  • "Don't save any history" mode, on demand or on a schedule
  • Groups, a panic button that closes listed tabs, and quick wipes
  • Share to Telegram, WhatsApp or Microsoft Teams
  • 9 interface languages, light and dark themes

The core features are free. HideHist Pro adds unlimited sites, groups, the schedule, the panic button and more for a one-time payment.
