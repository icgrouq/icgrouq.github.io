HideHist — Hide Your History

Chrome extension

Keep the sites you choose out of your browser history, pause history saving completely, wipe the last minutes in a tap, and share any page to Telegram in one click.

  • Exception list with domains, pages and patterns
  • "Don't save any history" mode for 30 minutes to 12 hours or until the browser closes
  • "Only listed sites" mode and quick wipes
  • English, Ukrainian and Russian interface, light and dark themes
